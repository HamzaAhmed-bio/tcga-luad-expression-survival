# Analysis plan v2: frozen before gene-level results

This supersedes implementation and cohort choices in v1; the research question is unchanged.

The installed workbench has no suitable existing-count differential-expression workflow and no local workflow controller. Use project-local Python scripts, PyDESeq2 0.5.4 (DESeq2 method implemented in Python), lifelines, and GSEApy. This is not a registered Workbench execution and does not claim exact numerical equivalence to R DESeq2. Dependencies are isolated and frozen.

Cohort rule: exclude every patient-condition group with multiple files or samples rather than arbitrarily select aliquots. Also exclude cases with annotation categories for neoadjuvant therapy, unacceptable prior treatment, changed qualification metrics, or protocol deviation. Preserve remaining prior/synchronous-malignancy notifications and examine them in sensitivity analysis. This yields 50 pairs and 496 unique primary tumors; 6 additional eligible normal files have no eligible tumor pair and are not downloaded.

Differential expression: all annotated Ensembl genes meeting >=10 unstranded counts in >=50 of the 100 paired samples; design ~ patient + condition; explicit Tumor versus Normal contrast, BH alpha 0.05, standard Cook's filtering, no count replacement. Assess design rank and fit convergence. PCA uses log2(size-factor-normalized counts + 1), labeled accordingly. No outcome-driven outlier removal. Report both FDR significance and descriptive |log2FC| >=1 counts.

Candidate rule fixed before results: protein-coding genes with unique nonempty symbols, baseMean >=100, FDR <0.05 and |log2FC| >=1; sort by absolute Wald statistic, then gene ID, take first 10. This is an expression-first candidate set, not a prognosis-optimized signature. Freeze and hash the list before joining survival outcomes or testing external expression.

Pathway analysis: Hallmark 2020 gene sets downloaded from Enrichr, preserve and hash actual GMT. Rank tested genes by signed Wald statistic; for duplicate symbols retain highest baseMean, independent of significance; exclude nonfinite statistics. GSEApy prerank, 1,000 permutations, seed 20261005, min size 15, max 500; report enrichment FDR. Gene-set permutation inference is exploratory and can be affected by intergene correlation.

Survival source: original 2018 TCGA-CDR Supplemental Table S1 from its official GDC publication page. Use curated OS and OS.time rather than reconstructing incomplete current follow-up fields; join exact patient barcodes. This is a historical outcome freeze, not current follow-up. Age and stage from the same resource. Primary model: per-SD log2(TPM+1) expression + age/10 + stage (I, II, III, IV) categorical; complete-case analysis, one gene per model; BH across all 10 genes (unavailable tests count as p=1). Continuous expression, no optimized dichotomy. Check rank-transformed Schoenfeld tests, expression quadratic term as a nonlinearity sensitivity, and sensitivity excluding notification-positive cases. Significant results remain exploratory; no predictive performance claims. Descriptive KM uses cohort median only and displays risk counts.

Independent cohort: GSE31210, GPL570 microarrays, 226 primary lung adenocarcinomas and 20 normal tissue samples. Preserve original MAS5 normalization and log2-transform positive intensities; never treat arrays as RNA-seq counts. Map uniquely annotated probes to symbols; average log2 intensities across multiple unambiguous probes per symbol, without outcome-based probe selection. External expression testing is a Welch comparison between tumor and normal; normal-patient matching is not established, so label independence as an assumption and do not pool with TCGA.

For GEO survival honor the submitter's exclusion for incomplete resection/adjuvant therapy; require positive days before death/censor and dead/alive status. Adjust expression Cox models for age and stage I/II only if events support estimation; require >=10 deaths per coefficient, otherwise report unadjusted models with explicit limitation. Standardize expression within each cohort: replication is of association direction, not transport of a predictive model. BH across 10 prespecified candidates separately for external expression and external survival. A gene replicates survival association only if TCGA and external adjusted models meet FDR <0.05 with matching HR direction and acceptable diagnostic review. Report failures, unmapped genes, and violations. Distinct recruitment sources support cohort independence; individual-level overlap cannot be proven from deidentified records.

Sources: GDC mRNA pipeline documentation; PyDESeq2 documentation; GDC PanCan-Clinical-2018 publication page; GEO GSE31210 matrix and GPL570 annotation; GSEApy documentation. No claim of novelty, causality, or clinical validity.

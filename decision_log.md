# Execution and scientific decision log

1. The user authorized completion of the whole project. Data download, local isolated dependencies, computation, figures, and reporting were performed; nothing was submitted to a university or published externally.
2. The initial R implementation was replaced by PyDESeq2 0.5.4 because R was not discovered and existing Workbench workflows were for raw sequencing inputs. The paired negative-binomial model and contrast were retained. This was a local scripted analysis, not a Workbench registered run.
3. Cohort selection excluded ambiguous patient-condition groups and flagged quality/treatment categories before gene results. No outcome-based sample choice was used.
4. GDC sample-level clinical follow-up was incomplete. The official, historical 2018 TCGA-CDR outcome table was selected before survival modeling, instead of combining incomplete sources or guessing follow-up times.
5. All 546 downloaded files passed MD5 checks and use the same workflow version and GENCODE v36 header. Source-center barcode is 07 for all selected files. Eleven of the 50 paired patients have different plate barcodes between tumor and normal. Plate is not an independently established sequencing batch; remaining technical confounding is a limitation.
6. Paired PyDESeq2 reported convergence failures in 61 of 19,026 filtered genes (at least one fitting stage). Their original adjusted p-values remain in padj_before_convergence_gate; supported padj is missing for these genes. The original multiplicity correction is retained conservatively for successful fits. Failed-fit genes are excluded from candidate selection and pathway ranking. All ten candidates pass every convergence flag.
7. Candidate identities were fixed by the v2 expression-only rule before survival and external tests. No survival-optimized cutoff, probe selection, or signature fitting was used.
8. An additional sensitivity check compares the ten frozen genes by paired Wilcoxon tests on log2(normalized counts+1), separately in all 50 pairs and 39 same-plate pairs. This is an explicitly post-fit technical robustness check, not a new discovery screen or replacement for the main model; ten-test BH correction is applied within each subset.
9. GSE31210 reports 22 tumors excluded from prognosis analysis because of incomplete resection or adjuvant therapy; they are excluded here. Duplicate, inconsistently labeled month fields in GEO are ignored in favor of the clearly named death/censor days field.
10. The report must retain negative survival findings and diagnostic flags. Expression replication alone does not establish prognostic or clinical biomarker validity. Raw-read quality, tumor purity, detailed treatment, and comprehensive batch adjustment were outside the available-count analysis.
11. TCGA stage terms showed nominal proportional-hazards concerns. A post-fit sensitivity analysis stratified the baseline hazard by stage while adjusting for age; no candidate passed ten-test BH FDR 0.05. The primary and sensitivity results are both retained.
12. The complete analysis stages ran successfully. Reported quantities and BH calculations were checked against saved tables, and figures were visually inspected. The portable Windows reproduction entry point was syntax-checked; a second clean installation and full rerun of the packaged project were not performed.
